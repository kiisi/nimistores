About Us

A Taste of Home in Gloucester

Nimistores is a destination for authentic African groceries and everyday essentials. Located in the heart of Gloucester at Morelands Trading Estate, we bring the flavours and ingredients of home closer to you.

Whether you're looking for pantry staples, speciality spices, or those hard-to-find ingredients that make your dishes truly authentic, Nimistores is here to serve you with care and quality.
